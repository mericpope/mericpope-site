Whistlestop

The cheapest way there — even if it starts somewhere else.

“To start” is how many hours you’re willing to spend travelling from your starting point to reach a departure point (taxi, train, bus, ferry — or a short flight to another city). “To finish” is the same from where you land to your destination. Airports in your starting city and your destination city (JFK, LaGuardia and Newark for New York) are always allowed — the hours are for going beyond them, such as landing in Washington and taking a train. Time spent waiting at airports isn’t counted against these, but it is shown in each trip’s total. The date is the day of the main flight; with an overnight stay, travel before the hotel night happens the day before (and after it, the day after you land).

Acceptable ways to travel

Budget airlines charge for anything beyond a small personal item, so the headline fare can be much lower than what you’ll pay. “1 cabin bag” re-prices every flight with the bag included, from live data. Checked-bag fees are not available from the price source, so they are never guessed: trips are flagged instead, and each separate ticket means another fee.

More options

How accurate is this? Flights are shown only when they have a real Google Flights quote (marked LIVE, with airline and flight number); if live prices can't be fetched, no flights are shown. Prices marked EST — taxis, trains, buses, ferries and hotels without a live rate — are estimates, because there is no real-time source for them. Train, bus and ferry timetables aren’t checked, and “set off by” times only appear when real flight times are available. “Find this flight” opens that day’s results for the exact route and airline; pick the flight number shown. Always confirm before booking. Flights cover airports worldwide; train, bus and ferry coverage is currently limited to the UK, Western Europe and the US north-east, Chicago and California.
