Trip
My points

Airlines don’t let anyone read award prices automatically, and Google doesn’t show them. So you type in the points price you find on the airline’s site, on the flight in a trip, and Whistlestop compares it with the cash price everywhere that flight appears. Everything here stays in this browser.

Balances are optional. With one entered, only what you can afford is offered; without one, a program is assumed to have enough.

Ranking

How accurate is this? Flights are shown only when they have a real Google Flights quote (marked LIVE, with airline and flight number); if live prices can’t be fetched, no flights are shown. Prices marked EST — taxis, trains, buses, ferries and hotels without a live rate — are estimates, because there is no real-time source for them. Train, bus and ferry timetables aren’t checked, and “set off by” times only appear when real flight times are available. “Find this flight” opens that day’s results for the exact route and airline; pick the flight number shown. Always confirm before booking. Flights cover airports worldwide; train, bus and ferry coverage is currently limited to the UK, Western Europe and the US north-east, Chicago and California. Airport data: OurAirports (public domain).
