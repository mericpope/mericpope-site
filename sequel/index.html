Sequel is a dating service that takes people from a first date to a relationship, not just to a match. Every match comes with a planned date, every planned date ends with a private check-in, and every mutual “yes” unlocks ideas for the next date.

Private answers

Used only for matching and planning, never shown to a match: timing, age range, travel, dating a parent, five years, last relationship, how much faith and politics matter, budget, food needs and the secret wishlist.

Firm filters

  • Who each person wants to meet
  • Both people’s age ranges
  • “A relationship” never meets “Not sure yet”
  • Wants kids never meets doesn’t want kids
  • A parent never meets someone who won’t date one

Soft signals

Faith and politics never block a match. They lower its score only when someone said they matter and the views differ. Suggestions rank by shared loves and favourites, the same idea of a good date, pace and overlapping free time.

Matching

The weekly shortlist: choose dates, not faces

Sequel has no swiping. Once a week you get up to four cards, each a date at a time you’re both free, with a person. You pick, you rank, and one matching round sets up at most one first date each.

The card

The plan leads: what, where, when and travel time for each of you. Then photos, prompts, fact chips and what you share. Plans suit the slot, such as an evening for a play or a weekend daytime for a market.

The round

  • Mutual “I’d go”s only
  • Rankings settle clashes fairly
  • One date per person per week
  • A near miss gets priority next week

No visible rejection

No match reads “No date this week. Matching depends on timing as much as anything.” Three weeks of saying yes without a date gets a person shown more widely.

  • Your usual week: a daytime and evening grid in the questionnaire, which the Friday check can change for one week.
  • Pause any time, one tap. “Not this week” is a one-week pause, and “Just us” switches the shortlist off for both people.
  • After “It’s on”, messages open for the logistics. Dates 2 and beyond don’t wait for Sunday.
  • During the test, each week’s shortlists are reviewed by hand before they go out.

  • Both say yes: “You both said yes”, and date 2 unlocks.
  • Anything else: a kind, pre-written closure. No reasons, no scores. Every other answer stays private.
  • Always a safety exit: “I felt unsafe” skips the check-in and reports straight away.
  • 72 hours to answer, then the match closes and the missing person’s reliability score drops.

Dates 2 and beyond

Sequel plans alongside you

  • An arc, not a random list: date 2 is easy, dates 3–4 more personal, later dates bigger shared experiences.
  • Fitted to both people: budget, drinks, pace and taste.
  • Wishlist hints: one per date, shown as “We think Sam would love this”, never in their own words.
  • Turn-taking: whoever didn’t plan the last date plans the next.
  • A question for each stage, light at first and deeper later.

Becoming a couple

Good news or no news

Just us?

After three good dates: “Are you still seeing other people?” Private answers, revealed only if both say yes. A mutual yes switches off the weekly shortlist for both.

Make it official?

At 6 dates or 8 weeks, if things look good and you’re still seeing each other. Either person can start it any time. Both yes: it’s official, and the couple moves into Resonance.

When it isn’t yes

“Not yet” changes nothing and nobody is told. Stepping back is a separate, kind ending. After three not-yets, both get a private, gentle check-in about their own needs.

Safety and privacy

Check-ins are the most sensitive thing Sequel holds

  • Report and leave any time, with no check-in needed. Reported people are never matched with you again and can’t see your photos.
  • During the test, every member is vouched for by someone who knows them. Photo and ID verification come before any public launch.
  • Never browsable: no desirability score anyone can look up. Check-in answers are never shown to the other person.
  • Data: deleted on request; how long check-ins are kept after a match ends will be limited.

Launch

Small first, then earn the next step

  1. Invite-only friend test (now): about 30 friends of friends in one city, with weekly shortlists reviewed by hand for six weeks.
  2. Sequel by text: notifications and reminders, then texting between matches.
  3. Culture Ledger link: real ratings instead of typed favourites.
  4. Couple handoff into Resonance.
  5. Its own app, once one city has enough active members.

Each step only goes ahead if the one before shows people actually go on the dates and come back.
